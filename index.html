Mindo brings traditional Indian board games and chess together in one app. Play against the computer or share your device with a friend in Pass & Play mode. Available Games Game Description Mindo: 18 Goti Plan your moves, capture opposing pieces, and outplay your opponent in this traditional Indian strategy game. Mindo Ludo Roll the dice and race your pieces home. Mindo Chess Play against the computer or a friend, or practise with interactive lessons. Mindo Saanp Seedhi Climb ladders, watch out for snakes, and race to the finish. Coming Soon Carrom, Anthi Chanda, Chauka Bara, Three Men’s Morris, and Nine Men’s Morris.
