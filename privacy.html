Mindo Privacy Policy

Mindo is an offline board-game app developed by Abhinav Shukla. Mindo stores your display name, preferences, game progress and statistics on your device so you can resume games and see your results. Mindo does not require an account. This version does not send game data to a Mindo server or share it with advertising partners.

You can remove the app’s local data using Android Settings → Apps → Mindo → Storage → Clear data. Android may back up or restore eligible app data according to your device and account settings. You can manage those backups in Android settings.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and troubleshoot, and retain it as needed to handle the request. Please avoid including passwords or sensitive information.

For privacy questions or deletion of a support email, contact contact.saraagames@gmail.com.
